throw away. If we find anything good in other countries
we should certainly take it.

As Indians we have to live in India and work for India.
But we must not forget that we belong to the larger family
of the world and the people living in other countries are
after all our cousins. It would be such an excellent thing
if all the people in the world were happy and contented.
We have therefore to try to make the whole world a happier
place to live in. |
