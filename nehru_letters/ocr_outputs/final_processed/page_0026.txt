raised himself and became more and more powerful till he
became stronger than any animal. How did he do this?
Not by physical strength for the elephant is much stronger
than he is. It was by intelligence and brain power.

We can trace this growth of intelligence from the earliest
days of man to the present day. Indeed it is intelligence
that separates man from the other animals. There is
practically no difference between a man without any
intelligence and an animal.

The first great discovery that man probably made was |
that of fire. We light a fire now by a match. But of course
matches are quite recent things. In olden times fires were
made by rubbing two flints against each other till a spark
came, and this spark set fire to a piece of dry straw or some
other dry thing. Fires sometimes occur by themselves in
the forests perhaps by the rubbing together of flints or
something else. The animals were not clever enough to
learn anything from this. But man was cleverer. He saw
the use of fire. It kept him warm in the winter and fright away his enemies, the big animals. So whenever a fire
