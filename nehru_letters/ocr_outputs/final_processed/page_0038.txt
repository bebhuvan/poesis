English in this way, and English has borrowed some words

been borrowed. They cannot be new words. Right at the
beginning when people started talking to each other, there
were of course fathers and mothers and words must have

words are not borrowed. They must have come down
from the same ancestor or the same family. And from this
we can find out that the people living far apart now in
different countries and using different languages must have
belonged once upon a time to the same big family.
