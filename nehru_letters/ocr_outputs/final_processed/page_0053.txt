died they remained in his family. We see how the idea of
anything belonging to ‘me’ or ‘you’ started. There was
no such idea to begin with. People all worked together for
the tribe and not for themselves. If they produced aq lot ot
food or other things everybody in the tribe got his share.
There were no rich or poor in the tribe. They were all
sharers in the property of the tribe.

But as soon as the patriarch started grabbing at the things
belonging to the tribe and calling them his own we begin
to get rich people and poor people.
