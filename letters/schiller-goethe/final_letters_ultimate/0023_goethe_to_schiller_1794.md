# Letter 23: Goethe to Schiller

**Metadata:**
- **Location:** Weimar
- **Date:** 1794

---

To-morrow, by ten o'clock, I hope to arrive at Jena

with Meyer, and to pass some pleasant days in your neighborhood. I hope I shall find you well.
