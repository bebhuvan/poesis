# Letter 56: Goethe to Schiller

**Metadata:**
- **Location:** Weimar
- **Date:** May 16, 1705

---

Here you have, at last, my dear friend, the second

volume of Wilhelm. I wish him on his public appearance

the continuance of your favor. I am trying now to get the

fifth book in order, and as the sixth is already finished,

I hope before the end of this month to have worked myself free for the Summer. I hope soon to hear how you

are getting on.

The accompanying copies 1 beg you to distribute according to the subscription. Farewell.
