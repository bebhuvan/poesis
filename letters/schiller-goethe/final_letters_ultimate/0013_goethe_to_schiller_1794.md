# Letter 13: Goethe to Schiller

**Metadata:**
- **Location:** Weimar
- **Date:** 1794

---

That the editors of the Horen receive Mr. Zahn of

Tübingen, into their association, and allow him a consultative voice in the affairs which regard this monthly journal, seems to me perfectly suitable to the circumstances.

It is to be understood, that this connection lasts only so

long as Mr. Cotta is the publisher.
