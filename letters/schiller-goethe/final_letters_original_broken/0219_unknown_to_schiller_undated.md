# Letter 219: Unknown to Schiller

---

Zurich, 25th October, 179 7.

Before I leave Zlirich, only a few words ! for I am

very much pre-occupied, and shall, too, continue so for

awhile, for we think of going to Basle, thence to SchafThausen, Tuebingen, and so further ; probably at the latter

place, I shall again find something from you. No Almanac of the Muses, no Herrmann have I yet seen; all that,

and more besides, I suppose I shall meet with in Germany.

Were the season not so far advanced, I should like to

look about for a month longer in Switzerland, in order to
