# TO  THE  NATION.

TO THE NATION. 

In January 1932 the political situation in India 
was tense and explosive. The country was bubbling 
with enthusiasm of unprecedented intensity and people 
were bent upon casting away the yoke of slavery by hook 
or by crook. The Congress had already passed a resolution involving the resumption of Civil Disobedience 
Movement and on the other hand, the British Government were bent upon suppressing the upheavals by all possible means, and they had already apprised the Mahatma 
of their intentions through a telegram. At that critical 
period Mahatma Gandhi issued the following message to 
the Nation: —
