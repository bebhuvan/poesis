Letter CXXVII

W ardha
January 4, 1936

I am very unhappy about not being able to come

there, but I could not agree to the hard terms the doc¬

tors would have imposed on me. If my health is as bad

as they think, it is just as well that I do not go. Now

I wish that you would come here in a few days

after completing the collections for Harijans. Also bring

Rajendrababu. *You will probably collect the necessary

funds in Alimedabad alone.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
