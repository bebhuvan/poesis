Letter CCLXVII

Shahpur
January 14, 1947

... Now about the proposed Bihar Commission

of Inquiry.

Is it not sufficient to stop the Chief Minister that

you are yourself against a Commission as well as the

Governor and the Viceroy? In spite of all this, I am

strongly of opinion that if no commission is appointed,

the League’s report about the ill-treatment of Muslims

will be regarded as based on facts beyond dispute. I

should know about, what pressure is being put on me,

should not I?...

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
