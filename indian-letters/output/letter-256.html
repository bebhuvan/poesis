Letter CCLVI

Valmiki Mandir
Slip New Delhi

Monday, September 2, 1946

*You have been in my thoughts since the morning

prayers. Abolish the salt-tax; remember the Dandi

march, unite Hindus and Muslims, remove untouchability

and make Khadi your own.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
