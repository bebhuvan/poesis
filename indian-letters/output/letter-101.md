# Letter CI

**Wardha**  
_May 15, 1935_

I do not regard your joke about “the number of

crows in Delhi” as proper. What will it profit the

people? We are not campaigning at present.

I am bogged up in work just now. Andrews is here.

Ceresole and [Miss Ellen] Wilkinson came yesterday.

Others also will be coming. I will only just manage to

get ready on the 21st. It seems impossible to bring

Mahadev with me.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
