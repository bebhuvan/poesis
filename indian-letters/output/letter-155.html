Letter CLV

Segaon
July 19, 1938

Both these gentlemen are from the Quilon Bank

and wish to tell you their story and also to get your

advice; Do give them some time. Theirs is a tragic story.

They also want to see Sir Purushottamdas. I have

given them a note for him too.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
