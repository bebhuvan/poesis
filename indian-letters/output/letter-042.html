Letter XLII

Raibag
March 8, 1934

There is no letter from you lately. I have started

this letter before prayers. We left Belgarn yesterday.

Raibag is a small village but it is on the railway line.

I have been late in writing this time because Dahyabhai,

Ghandubhai, Ourga, Jivanji and others all came to

Belgarn when I was there. Dahyabhai met Mani, while

Durga, Jivanji and Babla saw Mahadev. Mani and

Mahadev can be said to be fairly well. Mahadev is

absorbed in his own work....

I have had a full report from Ghandubhai [about

conditions in Nasik prison]. Do persevere with neti,

taking care to use fine cloth.

Lester left for Delhi. Harrison is expected to arrive

on the 1 6th. Ba5s letter is enclosed herewith....

I. shall reach Patna on the nth. Thakkarbapa and

his entourage wih go to Delhi. I shall call him over

iater if there is any possibility of continuing the Harijan

tour after reaching Patna.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
