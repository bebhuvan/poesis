Letter CCLXXI

Shrinagar (Bengal)
February 5, 1947

... I made a long speech about the League. You

have perhaps read it m the newspapers. It bummarizes

my views on the subject.

L. S~i4

I take the Cabinet Mission statement to mean

that there is nothing to fear if even the princes do not

join the Constituent Assembly. Nothing is lost even if

they do not interpret it in the same way. And if they do,

it will only show them in a better light, and we shall

be able to get to work directly. It is as clear as day¬

light to ine that there is no need to experience shortages

in lood and cloth. It is another matter if I cannot convince

others about it. In such a position it does not make much

difference whether I come there or do not. My place

is here only. I am satisfied with what I can do here. I

believe that I bring some slight happiness to the people

here, and I may be able to do more if I continue to

function. But that is in the hands of Providence.

I hear that your opposition is reported to be the

reason why the Bihar Ministry does not appoint an

inquiry commission, I do not believe it, but I should bring

it to your notice. If a commission is not appointed,

much damage will be done. The Ministry will be regarded

as guilty. If their work has been above board, what harm

can the Commission do to them? Considerable pressure

is being exerted on me, but I did not go because I reposed

confidence in the Ministers. But if a Commission is not

appointed after all, it seems to me that I shall have

to go to Bihar.

I hope you are taking sufficient care of your health.

The Sardar's Reply

Who told you that I was responsible for the non-

appointment of a Commission in Bihar? I think that

there is nothing to be gained by appointing it and that

there will be some harm on the other hand. But if

it is to be appointed, how can I stand in their way?

It is surprising to see that all these false stories come to

you. Why do not those who tell them to you come to me ?

Such persons who talk behind people’s backs must be

exposed.

The Bihar Governor is responsible for the non-

appointment. The Viceroy also would not like it to

be instituted. Otherwise who can prevent it? The Calcutta

Commission was appointed at the instance of the Viceroy.

They are still making inquiries. It will be twelve months

before they report. What use will the report be after

such a long period? Only the money spent on it will

be wasted. I cannot see the reason why the odium

should be placed on me.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi
