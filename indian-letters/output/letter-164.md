# Letter CLXIV

**Segaon**  
_December 21, 1938_

The Maulana refuses flatly [ to accept Presidentship

of the Congress]; it is not therefore advisable to press

him further. I feel it would be better if we consider

Pattabhi [Sitaramayya].


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
