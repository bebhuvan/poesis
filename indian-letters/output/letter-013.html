Letter XIII

May 9> *933
I passed a good night. This place [Parnakuti] is

more airy and much cooler than the prison. I slept in

the open. Here there is more work to do. But that will

be for two days only. After that I have decided to stop

working altogether. I do not feel any particular weakness

just yet. Don’t worry at all. Your loving care of me

was as tender as that of a mother.

[Written to the Sardar in Yeravda prison on the

second day of the 21 days’ fast for self-purification.]

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
