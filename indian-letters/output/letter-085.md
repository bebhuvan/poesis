# Letter LXXXV

**Wardha**  
_February 14, 1935_

You see I am giving my right hand a rest. I have

your letter as well as the account of the interview

[with the Home Member of the Government of India],

Quite a good interview. Keep up the correspondence

now.

I hope the nose does not hurt veiy much. When

are you coming here? Please fix a date for it. Discuss

things fully with Pyarelal.

I have taken on the kitchen now. My wTork has

changed and increased beyond expectations. But that

cannot oe a matter for complaint. Mahadev comes

tomorrow.

[This and the following were sent to Bombay.]

9%


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
