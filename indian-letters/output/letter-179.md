# Letter CLXXIX

**Sevagram**  
_May 13, 1940_

I have already written to you about this. I also

wrote to him. I imagine you know that Nanabhai is

in it. We shall have to send Rs 2,000 for the present.

We will manage. I am writing to him in detail. You

also please write.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
