# Letter CCXLIII

**Uruli-kanchan**  
_March 22, 1946_

A letter from the X iceroy asking me to see him on

the third was received this afternoon. 1 have not yet

replied but I shall have to go.

The meeting about the Indians in South Africa is

to be held on the evening. It will be convened by

the Imperial Citizenship Association and I am to be

the piesident. You will of course hear more about it

there.

It.wenis that a fair beginning has been made here;

the end we shall know later on. I do not think your

pessimism is justified.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
