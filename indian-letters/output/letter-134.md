# Letter CXXXIV

**Segaon, Wardha**  
_May 4, 1937_

Where do you propose to take me? V/herever you

take me, you will have to provide for a large number

of persons, as X shall not be able to prevent any one

from coming. X do not mind it at all, but we must think

of those in whose bungalow we are put up. Miraoehn

has already served a notice that she is to accompany

me this time wherever X go. X do not feel that I need

seaside air myself. You should definitely keep me in

Bardoli as long as you like ; so also in Surat. If you do

not object to our becoming a large party, do not assume

that X would be bothered by it. X would oi course be

very reluctant to become a heavy burden. This is

the list up to now :

Ba, Kano, Mira, Pyarelal, Mahadev, Radha-

krishna, ICanu, Manharlal, bharacia.

X. hope you are taking sufficient rest.

GXXXV

Wardha

June 19, 1937

.... I read about the affair in Broach. Such

lies. will continue to be told. What other type of be¬

haviour could you expect them to adopt towards some

one like Dinkarrao ?

I hr Working Committee can meet from the 26th

to the 29th. That gives us sufficient time, and no doubt

the earlier it is held, the better it will be.

_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
