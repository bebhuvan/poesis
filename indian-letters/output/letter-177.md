# Letter CLXXVII

**Patna**  
_May  17,  1947_

I  got  your  and  Jawaharlal’s  letters.  I  feel  no  enthu¬

siasm  at  all  to  go  to  Mussoorie.  You  should  stay  in

Mussoorie  as  long  as  you  can.  Whatever  days  I  can  get

here  will  be  spent  usefully;  so  if  you  agree,  I  shall  reach

LETTERS  TO  SARDAR  PATEL

Delhi  on  the  31st;  or  any  time  after  that  as  you  wish.

I  would  like  you  to  take  complete  rest  in  Mussoorie.

Talks  we  shall  have  in  Delhi.

I  read  about  Darbar  Gopaldas  in  the  newspapers.

1  was  sure  about  it  [restitution  of  his  estate  to  him].  There

was  a  telegram  from  him  today;  so  I  wrote  to  him.

_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
