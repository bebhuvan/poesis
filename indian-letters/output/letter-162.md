# Letter CLXII

**Segaon**  
_(December  1938)_

I  read  the  papers  through.  It  is  a  shocking  state

of  things.  If  the  Thakoresaheb  remains  firm  a  settlement

can  be  reached  at  once.  But  I  have  my  doubts  about

his  firmness.  How  far  can  we  make  use  of  the  information

found  in  these  papers?  If  you  are  invited  you  should

certainly  go.  I  feel  that  if  you  go  there  you  should  also

see  the  Resident  and  speak  to  him  frankly.  The  invi¬

tation  from  the  Raja  cannot  be  kept  a  secret.  If  the

Raja  does  not  have  so  much  courage,  it  may  perhaps

not  be  worth  while  going  to  Rajkot.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
