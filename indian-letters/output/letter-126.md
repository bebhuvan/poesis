# Letter CXXVI

**SegaonJ**  
_May  1,  1936_

Mahadev  cannot  start  today.  One  of  the  reasons

for  this  has  been  stated  in  my  telegram.  The  other  is  that

he  has  to  send  matter  for  the  week’s  Harijan.  If  this  is

done  before  he  goes,  you  can  detain  him  even  longer.

Mahadev  will  tell  you  all  about  this.

I  should  warn  you  that  if  you  do  not  bring  your

health  to  normal,  there  will  be  a  quarrel.

The  climate  of  this  village  is  really  good.  The

night  was  beautifully  cool.  There  are  fairly  good  arrange¬

ments  about  the  meals  etc.  More  about  that  at  leisure.

Dr  Ambedkar  and  Valchand  met  me  once  and  are

coming  again.
