# Letter XXXVII

**Kunur**  
_January 30, 1934_

I have not yet received your letter. I am writing'

this at two p.m. basking in the Kunur sunshine. Before

this I finished the weekly articles for Harijan, lunched,

finalized the programme for Tamiinad and had a nap.

Bihar takes much of my time at present. You must

have read about the distress prevailing there. I get daily

reports from Rajendrahabu as regards the organization

of relief. 1 am doing whatever things he wants me to do,

and there is no immediate need for me to go there. He

has asked for the help of those Ashramites who have

een re^eased- I have already sent telegraphic instructions

anu ail such as can will go. I have not yet been informed

as to who will go. I refer to Bihar in every meeting, and

have also collected some cash and ornaments. The response

to my appeal is quite good. We have yet to decide how

exactly the collections are to be used.

AmtuI Salam is here. She was ready to start even

today, but I detained her for a week, after which she

Wlt ^ack to her post in Gujarat. Gangabehn and

others are recuperating.
