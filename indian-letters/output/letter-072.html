Letter LXXII

Wardha
October 4, 1934

I had your letter yesterday and asked Kishorlal to

write to you. I am very busy and just manage to cope

with the work although something always remains in

arrears at the end of the day.

Aney met me yesterday. Nekiram had come the

day before. I suggested to Aney that he and you could

still talk things over, and if there is any difference of

opinion, some such person should be appointed um¬

pire, as for instance Bahadurji or Tejbahadur [Sapru].

But he did not favour my proposal. He said that the

names would keep changing until the scrutiny was com¬

pleted and whatever could be done would have to wait

until then.

Then again Aney said it would be better if the elec¬

tion were held in November. I said : ‘"Vallabhbhai

refused an extension only for Malaviyaji’s sake. If you

send a telegram to Vallabhbhai he may perhaps grant

the extension.” I do not know if this is at all feasible.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
