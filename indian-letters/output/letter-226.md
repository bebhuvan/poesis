# Letter CCXXVI

**Sevagram**  
_August 3, 1945_

I had intended to leave here on the 8th and to take

you with me to Poona on the 10th. But I now find that

I am tied up with meetings until the 19th, so the earliest

I can start is on the 19th. I do not like it as I wanted

to get away as soon as you were free. Now you will have

to carry on for ten days more. You may stay a little

longer in Ahmedabad if you wish. The best thing would

be for you to come and stay in the Ashram for the

TRY POONA 17 I

intervening period, and then we could go to Poona

together....

As for the Mahadev memorial, it would not be

proper for me to issue a public appeal for funds. I might

write to a few persons. It does not matter if Bombay

does nothing about it. Do go over the plan I have suggested.

More about it later or when we meet.

[This was sent to Ahmedabad.]

_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
