# Letter CXXXVI

**Scgaon,**  
_June 21, 1937_

1 went through your letter and Jawahar’s reply.

11 appears that Nariman will himself fall into the pit

he is digging for others.

I have received a long letter from Lothian. I have

not yet been able to read it. I hope you are sufficiently

recovered to move about* in the house.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
