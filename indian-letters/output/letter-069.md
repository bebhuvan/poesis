# Letter LXIX

**Wardha**  
_... Kaka is down with fever. The temperature has_

not yet returned to normal. He has nothing else apart

from cold and a little cough. There still is the possibility

of typhoid. Do write a note to Kaka.

Jones is better but not quite well. Please write

him also a couple of lines. Dr Khansaheb is examining

him....


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
