# Letter LXXXI

**Wardha**  
_December  23,  1934_

You  must  have  seen  the  resolutions  passed  by  the

Millowners5  Association.  See  that  they  do  not  start

unnecessary  quarrels;  if  any  mill  owner  is  likely  to  listen

to  you,  speak  to  him.  I  wrote  to  Kasturbhai  and

Charnanbhai.

Please  keep  mentioning  the  Village  Industries

Association  wherever  you  go;  for  it  has  great  poten¬

tialities.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
