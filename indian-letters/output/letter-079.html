Letter LXXIX

Wardha
December 13, 1934

Your letter.... I am unhappy about Karnatak.

But there is very little we can say in a place where there

is some one like Gangadharrao [Deshpande], Do what

you can.

I am deep in the Village Industries Association.

Rajaji has been here and he wants to leave today;

Jamnalal will come there in a few days.

ihe rest of the news from Mahadev.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
