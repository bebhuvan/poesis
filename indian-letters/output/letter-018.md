# Letter XVIII

**Wardha**  
_September 30, 1933_

A letter from Mani was received yesterday. She

appears to have an enlarged spleen and is taking some

treatment for it. So it will be some time before

she arrives here....

I hope Doctor Chandulal [Desai] is all right.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
