Letter LIV

Garadpur [Orissa]
June 7, 1934

I here is no letter from you yet this time also:

mine have all been sent regularly. It has started raining

here; so everything has now come to a standstill. It will

shortly be time for the morning prayer. While I was

writing tins, Satishbabu arrived with a party of ten

irom Bhadrak station with their luggage; it took them

an hour and three quarters to negotiate the two-mile

muddy road.

The prayers are over and I resume.

Satishbabu is touring Bengal on foot. It is difficult

to assess the results of my pilgrimage as a pedestrian

but I for myself am fully satisfied. Everything else seems

insipid.

Harrison has left for Bombay. I shall meet her there

again. She is a very kind woman, JVIuriel who has been

travelling with Jamnalalji will also meet me in Bombay.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
