GLXXIII

Nagpur

September 24, 1939

Do not tell Lilavati [Munshi] or Hansabehn [Mehta]

just yet about going to Rajkot. I have written to Perin-

behn [A. D. Naoroji] that she should go. I thought I

ought to write to her. Wadia has not yet declined. I

have written to Perin that she should hold herself in

readiness to go if Wadia could not go. i shall write to

you when she replies. 1 have asked for the reply to be.

sent to Simla by wire.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
