# Letter CLXXIV

**Segaon**  
_October 14, 1939_

Please read the enclosed letter and reply to the

writer after making inquiries. I have replied in brief

that he should write to the Minister, but that is not

enough. We should subject such tnings to cJosei scrutiny,

Kishorlal yesterday was telling me that you had

said: c<Bapu has placed us in Jawaharlal s charge 5 so we

must go on doing as he says.” I hope this was only a joke.

I have not handed you over to any one. There was

a lot of discussion yesterday and the day before among

those who had stayed on here. It will not do if all of you

do not exercise your independent judgment and shift

the responsibility for not doing it on me.

GLXXV

Segaon

October 31, 1939

Why do you keep falling ill like this? You must

maintain your health. Send news to me in Delhi by wire*
