Don’t fall ill again by working beyond your strength.

I forgot to write about Kaka’s resignation of mem¬

bership in all trusts. His decision was taken with my

consent. It was taken not in sorrow, but only as a matter

of duty. It was I who suggested that he should write

to you about it. The question of your authority never

occurred to me. Kaka was very much hurt by your

remarks about his failure to consult other trustees and

behaving as if such consultations had been held.
