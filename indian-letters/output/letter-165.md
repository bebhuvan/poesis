# Letter CLXV

**Segaon**  
_December 31, 1938_

You already #know Shambhushankar. Pie hopes to

get swaraj in Palitana. Shambhushankar is a man of

independent temperament. His ambition is that he will

attain his object entirely depending on God’s grace.

But he does expect to receive the blessings of his

elders. I have already told him that my blessings would

always be given if he could carry on his struggle with

indomitable faith. All votaries of truth and non-violence

carry such blessings in their own pockets in any case.

But that is not enough to satisfy him and he wants

your blessings too. Please listen to what he says and give

him your best wishes.


---
_Source: Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi_
