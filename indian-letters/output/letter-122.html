Letter CXXII

Wardha
September 15, 1935

I was worrying over Manilal Kothari’s telegram.

In the end, I wrote him a letter, a copy of which is

enclosed. It is quite in order for you to have detained

Mahadev there. I am going more and more rural every¬

day. My bullock cart wading through several inches

of mud has no notion of time whatever.

From Letters to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel by M.K. Gandhi

Published by Navajivan Publishing House, Ahmedabad (1957)
