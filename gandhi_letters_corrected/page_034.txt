But  this  you  cannot  do  unless  you  consider 
every  Indian  to  be  in  reality  your  equal  and  brother. 
I   ask  for  no  patronage.  I   merely  point  out  to  you, 
as  a   friend,  an  honourable  solution  of  a   grave 
problem.  The  other  solution,  namely  repression,  is 
open  to  you.  I   prophesy  that  it  will  fail.  It  has 
begun  already.  The  Government  has  already  impri-
