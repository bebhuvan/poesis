38  Famous  Letters  of  Mahatma  Gandhi. 
Under  every  system  of  Government,  total  prohibi- 
tion so  far  as  I   can  see  will  be  insisted  upon  by  the 
nation.  You  can  assist  the  growth  of  the  ever -rising 
agitation  by  throwing  in  the  weight  of  your  influence 
on  the  side  of  the  nation. 
I   am, 
Your  faithful  friend, 
M.  K.  GANDHI.
