This  I   might  be  able  to  do,  only  if  you  put  me

If  I   could  be  convinced  of  my  error  or  worse, 
of  which  you  are  evidently,  I   should  need  to  consult 
nobody,  so  far  as  my  own  action  is  concerned,  to 
make  a   full  and  open  confession  and  make  ample 
amends.  But  I   have  not  any  conviction  of  error.
