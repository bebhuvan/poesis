THE  SECOND  LETTER. 
The  follomng  is  the  text  of  Gandhiji’s  second  letter 
to  Lord  Irwin  drafted  on  the  eve  of  his  arrest : 
Dear  Friend, 
1   must  Commence  the  March 
God  willing,  it  is  my  intention   to  set  out 
for  Dharsana  and  reach  there  with  my  companions 
• ,   — and  demand  possession  of  the  Salt  Works.  The 
public  have  been  told  that  Dharsana  is  private 
property.  This  is  mere  camouflage.  It  is  effective- 
ly under  Government  control  as  the  Viceroy’s  house. 
Not  a   pinch  of  salt  can  be  removed  without  the 
previous  sanction  of  the  authorities. 
It  is  possible  for  you  to  prevent  this  raid,  as  it 
has  been  playfully  and  mischievously  called  in  three 
ways; — 
by  removing  the  salt  tax ; 
by  arresting  me  and  my  party  unless  the 
country  can  as  I   hope  it  will,  replace 
every  one  taken  away ; 
by  sheer  gondaism  unless  every  head  broken 
is  replaced  as  I   hope  it  will.
