The  first  letter; 
WHY  I   REGARD  THE  BRITISH  RULE 
AS  A   CURSE ! 
Dear  Friend, — Before  embarking  on  Civil 
Disobedience  and  taking  the  risk  I   have  dreaded  to 
take  all  these  years,  I   would  fain  approach  you  and 
find  a   way  out.  My  personal  faith  is  absolutely 
clear.  I   cannot  intentionally  hurt  any  thing  that 
lives,  much  less  fellow-human  beings  even  though 
they  may  do  the  greatest  wrong  to  me  and  mine. 
Whilst  therefore  I   hold  British  rule  to  be  a   curse,  I 
do  not  intend  to  harm  a   single  Englishman  or  any 
legitimate  interest  he  may  have  in  India. 
I   must  not  be  misunderstood.  Though  I   hold 
the  British  rule  in  India  to  be  a   curse,  I   do  not 
therefore  consider  Englishmen  in  general  to  be 
worse  than  any  other  people  on  earth.  I   have  the 
privilege  of  claiming  many  Englishmen  as  dearest 
friends.  Indeed  much  that  I   have  learnt  of  the  evil 
of  British  rule  is  due  to  the  writings  of  frank  and 
courageous  Englishmen  who  have  not  hesitated  to 
tell  the  unpalatable  truth  about  that  rule. 
And  why  do  I   regard  the  British  rule  as  a 
curse  ?
