TO  Mr.  M.  A.  JINNAH. 
In  the  year  1938  a   serious  attempt  was  made  by 
the  Congress  Leaders  to  come  to  an  understanding  with 
Mr.  Jinnah,  President  of  the  Muslim  League  of  India 
with  a   view  to  bring  about  unity  between  the  two  organiza- 
tions— the  League  and  the  Congress— -and  to  solve  the 
Hindu-Muslirn  tangle.  Mr.  Jinnah  however  insisted  that 
an  essential  precondition  to  any  agreement  between  the 
two  organizations  was  the  recognition  by  the  Congress 
that  the  Muslim  League  was  the  sole  authoritative  and 
representative  political  organization  of  the  Muslims  of 
India.  The  talks  ended  in  smoke.  In  the  course  of
