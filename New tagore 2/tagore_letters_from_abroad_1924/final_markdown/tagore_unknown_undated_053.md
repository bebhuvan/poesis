For the same reason, when I used to have my 
dwelling on the bosom of the great river Padma, I 
was nothing more than a lyrical poet. But since I 
have taken my shelter at Santiniketan, I have 
developed all the symptoms of growing into a 
schoolmaster, and there is grave danger of my 
ending my career as a veritable prophet ! Already 
everybody has begun asking me for ‘ messages ’ ; and 
a day may come when I shall be afraid to disappoint 
them. For when prophets do appear unexpectedly
