# Life and Letters of Toru Dutt - Digitized E-Book

## About This Project

This is a digitized version of **"Life and Letters of Toru Dutt"** (1921) by Harihar Das, with a foreword by H. A. L. Fisher. The text has been extracted from the original scanned book at Archive.org, cleaned of OCR errors, and formatted into a high-quality e-book.

### About Toru Dutt (1856-1877)

Toru Dutt was a remarkable Indian poet who wrote in English, French, and Sanskrit. Despite her tragically short life—she died at only 21 years old—she left behind an extraordinary literary legacy that earned her recognition among the great poets of the 19th century.

Her works include:
- **Ancient Ballads and Legends of Hindustan** - English poetry based on Indian epics
- **A Sheaf Gleaned in French Fields** - Translations of French poetry
- **Le Journal de Mademoiselle d'Arvers** - A French novel
- **Bianca** - Poetry and prose

This biography includes her correspondence, particularly her letters to Mary E. R. Martin, which reveal her character, literary development, and the challenges she faced as an Indian woman poet in the Victorian era.

## Processing Details

### OCR Corrections Applied

The following improvements were made to the original OCR text:

1. **Character-level corrections**:
   - Fixed common OCR errors (e.g., "Torn" → "Toru", "wo" → "we")
   - Corrected misrecognized characters (e.g., "^" artifacts removed)
   - Fixed French accent marks (e.g., "6toit" → "était")

2. **Structural cleaning**:
   - Removed library stamps and catalog information
   - Removed page numbers and running headers
   - Cleaned up hyphenation across line breaks
   - Normalized spacing and formatting

3. **Formatting**:
   - Added proper chapter headings
   - Created hierarchical structure with sections
   - Preserved original dedication and epigraphs
   - Maintained paragraph structure and spacing
