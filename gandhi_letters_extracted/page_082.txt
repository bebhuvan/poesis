79 General Letter to all the Inmates. They have given me a man to clean vessels, etc., Dates and raisins I am taking. There is no reason to be anxious about my diet. There is, and ought to be, no time in these days for taking superfluous care of others. We have no money and ought to have no money for superfluous expenses. As for my spinning, it is regular. I make a daily bank of yarn. I never saw to my speed on takli outside the Jail. Interest should be created in spinning takli. In Wardha, some people have readied the speed of 80 rounds in half an hour. Let those who have learned there find out their exact speed and write to me. #
