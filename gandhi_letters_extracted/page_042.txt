TO THE YOUNGMEN OF BENGAL. When the Non-co-operation Movement of the years 1920, 1921  was in full swing, India needed the services of her youngmen more than ever before. Congress leaders and Ali Brothers made a joint appeal to the students to leave their studies and to join the volunteer corps in their thousands. The response from the Bengal youth was profoundly heartening. In other parts of India too stu- dents joined the Non-co-operation Movement in large numbers.
