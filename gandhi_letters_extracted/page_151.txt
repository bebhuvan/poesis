Dear Sir Richard, 1 have very carefully studied your letter- am sorry to say that there is nothing in the corres- pondence which has taken place between His Excel- lency and myself, or your letter to warrant a recall- ing of my intention to fast. I have mentioned tn letters to His Excellency the conditions which can induce prevention or suspension of the step. If the temporary release is offered for my convenience I do not need it. I shall be quite con- tent to take my fast as a detenu or prisoner. If it is for the convenience of the Government, I am sorry, I am unable to suit them, much as I should like to do so. I can say this much that I, as a prisoner, shall avoid, as far as is humanly possible, every cause of inconvenience to the Government sav^ what is inherent in the fast itself.
