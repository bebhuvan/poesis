# Letter 14

**Santiniketan, October 1914**

It seems as though I am coming out of the mist once 
more, and I am trying to throw off my shoulders the 
burden that has been oppressing me all these days. As my 
mind feels lighter, I hope I have rightly earned my 
freedom. 

We have all come to Santiniketan from Surul; and 
this change has done me good. Di. Maitra has sent me a 
long letter about you. He thinks you will have to be very 
careful in future about your health, if you are not to get 
ill again. 

Santiniketan, October th, 1914 
My period of darkness is over once again. It has been 
a time of very great trial to me, and I believe it was absolutely necessary for my emancipation. I know that I am 
being lifted from the sphere where I was before; and 
it is the loneliness of the new situation and the cry of 
the old life that is still troubling me. But I have glimpses 
of the ineffable light of joy, which I am sure will not 
fail me. Preaching I must give up, and also trying to take 
up the r61e of a beneficent angel to others. I am praying 
to be lighted from within, and not simply to hold a 
light in my hand.
