# Letter 8

**Ramgarh, May 18th 1914**

At last I am supremely happy, not simply because the
quiet of this place affords me the needful change from
the worries of a crowded life, but because it supplies my
mind with its natural food. Directly I come to a place
like this I can realize at once that I had been living before
on half-rations.

I have found myself since I came here, and I am filled
with the wonder that the infinite Power and Joy has
become what I am and what this blade of grass is. When
we are restless we raise dust all about us and we forget
the supreme truth that “we are.” I cannot tell you the
great joy of seeing everything through the sight which
comes from within.
