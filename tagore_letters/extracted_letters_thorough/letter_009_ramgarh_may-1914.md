# Letter 9

**Ramgarh, May 1914**

To-day is my father*s birthday anniversary. We have
just had our morning prayer, and my mind is full. It is
a stormy moining, dark and threatening, with an occaLetters to a Friend

sional burst of pallid light. It seems like the symbof of a
spiritual new birth. I have been experiencing the feeling
of a great expectation, although it has also its elements of
very great suffering. To be born naked in the heart of the
eternal Truth; to be able to feel with my enthe being
the life-throb of the universal heart — that is the cry of
my soul. I tell you all this, so that you may understand
what I am passing through and may help me when the
occasion arrives,

Do take care of yourself and get well, so as to
be fit to fight your own battle with renewed strength
and hope.
